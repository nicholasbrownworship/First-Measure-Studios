Second Soil

You wake up stranded on an alien planet with no memory of how you got there. The world is strange, beautiful, and trying to kill you — but the longer you survive, the more you realize something was here before you. And whatever it was, it left in a hurry.

Second Soil is a cooperative survival mystery game currently in development.

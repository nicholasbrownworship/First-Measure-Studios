Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 4, 2026

Introduction

First Measure Studios ("we", "us", or "our") operates Second Soil (the "Game"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Game.

Data We Collect

Second Soil uses Epic Online Services (EOS) to provide online multiplayer functionality. Through EOS, the following data may be collected:

  • Epic Games account identifier
  • Display name
  • Friends list (for session invites)
  • Online presence and game status
  • Session and matchmaking data

How We Use Your Data

Data collected through EOS is used solely to provide multiplayer functionality, including session creation, matchmaking, and friends list integration. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties outside of what is required to operate Epic Online Services.

Epic Online Services

Epic Online Services data collection and processing is governed by Epic Games' Privacy Policy, available at epicgames.com/privacypolicy.

Data Retention

We do not independently store personal data. All data processed through EOS is subject to Epic Games' data retention policies.
